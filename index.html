GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked
questions.

Can BusinessPilot AI send WhatsApp or Instagram messages now?

No. The first release provides the workspace and configuration foundation. Real messaging requires official platform permissions, credentials, webhooks and a configured backend.

Are AI tools live by default?

No. AI generation remains unavailable until a supported provider is configured securely on the server. Drafting screens remain available for manual work.

How is Premium activated?

Customers submit payment details and a transaction reference. An authorized administrator checks the bank credit independently before approving the request.

Can one business see another business's data?

No. Business data access is scoped by membership and enforced through Firestore security rules.
